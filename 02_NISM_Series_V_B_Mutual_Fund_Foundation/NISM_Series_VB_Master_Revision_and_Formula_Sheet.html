1. Complete Mathematical Formula Sheet (Chapters 1, 2, 5, 6 & 7)

Formula Name Mathematical Expression Exam Rule & Application
1. Future Value (Inflation Adjusted Goal) FV = PV × (1 + i)n Sapna Case: Rs. 10L at 8% for 10 yrs = Rs. 21.59 Lakh.
2. Net Asset Value (NAV) (Market Value of Assets − Liabilities) ÷ Total Units Unit Face Value is always Rs. 10; Unit Capital = Units × Rs. 10.
3. Total Expense Ratio (TER — April 1, 2026) TER = BER + Brokerage + Transaction Cost + Statutory Levies (incl. GST) Disclosed daily in Format No. 7E on AMC & AMFI websites.
4. Daily Trail Commission (Current Daily AUM × Trail Rate % p.a.) ÷ 365 100% Full Trail model; Upfront & transaction charges abolished.
5. Simple (Absolute) Return [(Later NAV − Initial NAV) ÷ Initial NAV] × 100 Used when investment holding period is ≤ 1 year.
6. Simple Annualized Return Simple Return × (365 ÷ Days) or × (12 ÷ Months) Used for Liquid/Overnight/MMF (7, 15, 30 days) & 6m–1yr schemes.
7. CAGR (Compounded Return) [(Ending Value ÷ Initial Value)(1 ÷ n) − 1] × 100 Mandatory for holding periods > 1 year (1, 3, 5 yrs & Inception).
8. Redemption Price (Exit Load) Redemption Price = Applicable NAV × (1 − Exit Load %) Exit load is credited back to the scheme (GST deducted from load).
9. Sharpe Ratio (Rs − Rf) ÷ Standard Deviation (σ) Excess return over 91-day T-bill (Rf) per unit of Total Risk.
10. Treynor Ratio (Rs − Rf) ÷ Beta (β) Excess return per unit of Systematic Risk (Market Beta = 1.0).
11. Information Ratio (IR) (Rp − Rb) ÷ Tracking Error (Std. Dev. of Excess Return) Mandatory daily disclosure on AMC & AMFI sites (Jan 17, 2025 circular).

3. Top 12 September 2026 Workbook Yellow-Highlighted Updates

  • 1. Sensex & AMFI 2026 Benchmarks: Sensex reached 82,269 on Jan 30, 2026 (~15.4% CAGR since 1979); Industry AUM touched Rs. 79.46 lakh crore and monthly SIP touched Rs. 32,087 crore (March 2026).
  • 2. SEBI Categorization Circular (26 Feb 2026): Introduced Life Cycle Funds (Category D, 5–30 yr glide path, 3%/2%/1% exit load) and Sectoral Debt Funds (17th debt category, min 80% in AA+ & above across 5 sectors); permitted AMCs to offer both Value and Contra Funds (max 50% overlap).
  • 3. Specialized Investment Fund (SIF): New category between MFs and PMS requiring a minimum investment of Rs. 10 Lakhs across all SIF strategies of the same AMC.
  • 4. SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 2026 & UHPC: Replaced the 1996 regulations; mandated a Unit Holder Protection Committee (UHPC) at the AMC level.
  • 5. Cyber Resilience (CSCRF & FIRE): AI task force cyber-suraksha.ai (May 2026) and FSB's FIRE framework (Aug 2026) mandating 6-hour email and 24-hour portal reporting.
  • 6. April 1, 2026 TER Formula: TER = Base Expense Ratio (BER) + Brokerage + Transaction Cost + Statutory Levies (incl. GST), disclosed daily in Format No. 7E.
  • 7. Full Trail Model & Abolition of Transaction Charges: Upfront commissions and Rs. 100/150 transaction charges completely withdrawn (Aug 2025 & March 2026 circulars).
  • 8. B-30 & Women Investor Commission (Nov 2025): 1% of first lump sum / 1st-year SIP up to Rs. 2,000 (1-yr lock-in) for new B-30 investors and new women investors (T-30 & B-30).
  • 9. Daily Information Ratio (IR) Disclosure: Mandatory daily disclosure of Information Ratio for all equity-oriented schemes (SEBI Circular Jan 17, 2025).
  • 10. Capital Gains Tax Rationalization (Effective July 23, 2024): Equity STCG = 20%, Equity LTCG (>12m) = 12.50% above Rs. 1.25 Lakh; Hybrid (35–65% equity) / FoF / International LTCG (>24m) = 12.50%; Debt (>65% debt) = Marginal Slab Rate.
  • 11. Overnight Fund Online Redemption Cut-Off (June 1, 2025): Extended to 7:00 PM for online applications (3:00 PM for physical).
  • 12. MITRA Portal, SOA/Demat Gifting & 10-Nominee Rule: QRTA MITRA portal traces inactive folios; SOA & Demat units can now be gifted to immediate relatives; nomination expanded up to 10 individuals (with optional incapacitation mandate).
