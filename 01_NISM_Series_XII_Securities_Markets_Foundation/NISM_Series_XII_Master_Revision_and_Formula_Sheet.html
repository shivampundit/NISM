PART 1: MASTER FORMULA CHEAT SHEET

1. Bond Valuation & Yield Formulas Fair Value of Bond (PV) = [ Coupon / (1 + y)^1 ] + [ Coupon / (1 + y)^2 ] + ... + [ (Coupon + Face Value) / (1 + y)^n ] Current Yield (%) = [ Annual Coupon (Rs.) / Current Market Price (Rs.) ] * 100

Exam Rules: If Market Yield > Coupon Rate → Bond trades at Discount (Price < Face Value). If Market Yield < Coupon Rate → Bond trades at Premium (Price > Face Value). Bond Price and Yield share an inverse relationship.

2. Equity Valuation Metrics Price-Earnings (PE) Ratio = Current Market Price per Share / Earnings per Share (EPS) Earnings per Share (EPS) = Profit After Tax (PAT) / Total Number of Outstanding Shares Price to Book Value (PBV) Ratio = Market Price per Share / Book Value per Share Book Value per Share = Net Worth (Paid-up Equity Capital + Free Reserves) / Number of Shares Dividend Yield (%) = [ Dividend per Share (Rs.) / Market Price per Share (Rs.) ] * 100

Exam Trap: Dividend % is declared on Face Value, while Dividend Yield compares Dividend to Market Price.

3. Primary Market Allotment & Rights Issue Math Oversubscription Ratio = Total Shares Applied For in Category / Shares Available for Category Shares Allotted to Applicant = Shares Applied By Applicant / Oversubscription Ratio Post-Rights Share Capital = Pre-Issue Capital + (Pre-Issue Capital * Rights Ratio)
4. Secondary Market Costs, Spreads & Impact Cost Bid-Ask Spread = Best Sell Price (Ask) - Best Buy Price (Bid) Ideal (Mid) Price = (Best Bid + Best Ask) / 2 Impact Cost (%) = [ |Execution Price - Ideal Price| / Ideal Price ] * 100 Turnover Ratio = Total Traded Turnover (in Rupees) / Total Market Capitalisation
5. Mutual Fund NAV & Transaction Math Net Asset Value (NAV) = [ (Market Value of Portfolio + Accrued Income) - (Accrued Expenses + Liabilities) ] / Total Outstanding Units Redemption Price (Repurchase Price) = Applicable NAV * (1 - Exit Load %) New NAV After Unit Creation = (Previous Net Assets + Fresh Cash Inflow) / (Previous Units + New Units Issued)
6. Derivatives Calculations & Ratios Basis (Futures) = Cash (Spot) Price - Futures Price Put-Call Ratio (PCR) = Total Outstanding Put Open Interest / Total Outstanding Call Open Interest Option Intrinsic Value (Call) = Max(0, Spot Price - Strike Price) Option Intrinsic Value (Put) = Max(0, Strike Price - Spot Price)

PART 4: RAPID MEMORY ANCHORS & TRAP BUSTERS

  • Primary Dealers (PDs): Regulated by RBI, not SEBI.
  • Commodity Spot Markets: Regulated by State Governments (APMC), while Commodity Futures/Derivatives are regulated by SEBI.
  • REITs vs. InvITs Classification: W.e.f. January 1, 2026, REIT units are classified as equity-related instruments for mutual funds, while InvITs remain hybrid.
  • Futures vs. Options Margins: Futures sellers AND buyers pay Initial Margin + Exposure Margin. For Options, Exposure Margin is levied only on short option positions (writers), while option buyers pay only the upfront premium.
  • Physical Settlement of Derivatives: Since October 2019, all individual stock futures and options are settled by physical delivery of shares. Index derivatives (Nifty, Bank Nifty) continue to be settled in cash.
  • Good-till-Cancelled (GTC) Orders: Not permitted on Indian exchanges. All unexecuted orders lapse at market close (Day Orders).
  • Passive vs. Active Orders: Limit orders are passive (provide liquidity to book); Market orders are active (drain liquidity).
